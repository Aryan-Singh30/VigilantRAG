Acme Corp General Guidelines & Policies:

1. Office Working Hours:
Our core working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday. 

2. Health & Wellness Insurance:
Acme Corp provides premium health insurance through BlueShield. Regular full-time employees are eligible for coverage starting on the first day of the month following their start date. Dental coverage includes 100% preventive care and up to $1,500 annually for minor/major dental services. Vision insurance covers one annual eye exam and a $200 allowance for frames or contact lenses every two years.

3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave:
Regular full-time employees accrue 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year, earned at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Sick leave is granted separately at 5 days per year and does not carry over.

4. Work Equipment and Internet Reimbursement:
Every remote and hybrid worker is provided with a MacBook Pro, an external 27-inch monitor, and a keyboard/mouse bundle. To support home office connectivity, Acme Corp reimburses up to $60 per month for home broadband internet costs. Receipts must be submitted through the ExpensePortal by the 25th of each month.

5. Learning & Professional Development:
We encourage continuous learning. Employees can claim up to $2,000 per calendar year for tuition, online courses (Coursera, Udemy), technical certifications, or conference tickets. Learning budgets must be pre-approved by your department head.
